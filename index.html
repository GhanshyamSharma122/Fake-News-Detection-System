03 / KEY VOCABULARY

Words behind the model

Use these terms in your presentation and report to explain the implementation precisely.

NLP

Natural Language Processing: methods that help computers work with human language.

Tokenization

Breaking a sentence into smaller units such as words or punctuation marks.

Feature

A measurable property of text used by a classifier, such as exclamation count.

Sentiment

The emotional polarity of language: positive, negative, or neutral.

Classifier

A model that maps input text to categories or risk levels.

Precision

Of the items predicted as suspicious, how many were actually suspicious.

Recall

Of all suspicious items, how many the system successfully detected.

Explainability

Showing understandable reasons behind a model output.

04 / INTEGRITY & ETHICS

Detection should create care, not certainty.

Language patterns can identify content worth checking, but they cannot prove whether a claim is true. NewsLens avoids collecting user text, shows evidence for its score, and asks people to consult trusted sources before sharing.

01

Bias awareness — training data may reflect political, cultural, or language bias.

02

Human review — a low score does not certify truth, and a high score does not prove falsity.

03

Privacy first — this demonstration performs analysis locally and does not store submissions.
